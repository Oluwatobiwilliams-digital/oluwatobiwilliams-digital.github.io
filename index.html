About me

I'm Oluwatobi Williams, a digital specialist based in Nigeria working with clients internationally. I've spent about three years in the online space, and websites are where I'm strongest. I build them in Webflow and WordPress, and I choose the platform that fits what the client needs, not the other way round.

Alongside websites, I help with landing pages, Shopify stores and Google Business Profile setup, so your online presence works as one system. If your project needs something different, tell me what you're trying to achieve and I'll say honestly whether I'm the right person for it.

I keep clients updated through the whole project, deliver on time, and stay open to feedback.
